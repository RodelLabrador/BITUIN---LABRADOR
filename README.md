Enhanced Simple E-commerce System

This project proposes the development of an enhanced simple e-commerce system designed to help small and medium-sized businesses in the Philippines establish a strong online presence. The system provides essential tools such as a product catalog, shopping cart, secure checkout, and order management to streamline online selling. By offering a user-friendly and secure platform, the system enables businesses to expand their market reach, improve efficiency, and deliver a convenient shopping experience for customers. Overall, it serves as a practical and accessible solution for businesses aiming to grow in the digital marketplace.
